Year One — 2026

12 landscapes.One constellation.

In year one, Kala Khoj uses the regions surrounding India's 12 Jyotirlingas as geographic anchors — selecting one artist from each landscape to form a diverse 12-artist cohort. Across these landscapes, the search will uncover:

  • Emerging contemporary visual artists
  • Regional and traditional art practices
  • Artists connected to colleges, collectives and community networks
  • Lesser-known materials and techniques
  • Underrepresented local and cultural narratives
