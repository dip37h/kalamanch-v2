What happens at Kalamanch?

The areas of activity the platform is being built around. Specific programmes, dates and participating artists will be published here as they are confirmed.

Curated presentations of visual art, giving finished and in-progress work room to be seen closely.

The practice and process behind an artist's work, presented as it takes shape rather than only as a finished piece.

Joint projects between artists, institutions and communities, built around a shared cultural idea.

Hands-on sessions where technique and craft are taught directly, practitioner to participant.

Dialogue-led sessions with artists and cultural thinkers on practice, context and meaning.

These represent the broader Kalamanch ecosystem rather than a confirmed calendar. The section is built so that live programmes and events can be added as they are announced.
